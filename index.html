Emergency Board-Up Service

When replacement glass can't be installed immediately—because of custom sizing, after-hours supplier closures, or fabrication lead time—board up is the responsible first step. We secure the opening against weather, debris, and unauthorized entry, then schedule the permanent repair.

Common reasons for board up: vandalism and break-ins, storm damage, delayed glass fabrication for oversized lites, and overnight security until morning replacement. Board up is available 24/7 across Baltimore and surrounding areas.

How Our Emergency Process Works

A clear, repeatable process so you know exactly what to expect:

1. Call & triage

Call 703-244-0559 any time, day or night. We ask for your address, access details, and a brief description of the damage to dispatch the right crew.

2. Fast dispatch

We send a crew with the tools and materials needed. ETA is typically 20–60 minutes depending on traffic, weather, and crew availability.

3. Secure & assess

On arrival, we stabilize the site—board up if needed, clean up loose glass safely, and assess the full scope of repair or replacement required.

4. Repair or schedule

If same-day repair is possible, we do it. If custom glass is needed, we schedule the install and keep the site secure until then.

FAQ

How fast can you arrive for emergency glass repair in Baltimore?

It depends on traffic, weather, and crew availability. In many calls across Baltimore City and Baltimore County, arrival can be as quick as 20–60 minutes. Call 703-244-0559 for a real-time ETA based on your location.

Do you do board up and replacement?

Yes. If replacement glass can't be installed immediately—due to custom sizing, after-hours supplier closures, or fabrication lead time—we secure the opening with a professional board up first and then schedule repair or replacement once materials are available.

Do you work on commercial storefronts and door glass?

Yes. We handle storefront glass, commercial entry door glass, sidelites, and display windows for retail stores, restaurants, offices, and property managers throughout Baltimore.

Do you offer free estimates?

For scheduled (non-emergency) work, we can often provide a free estimate once scope is confirmed with photos and measurements. For emergencies, we prioritize securing the site first and then quote the full repair or replacement scope.

What areas do you serve around Baltimore?

We serve Baltimore City neighborhoods including Downtown, the Inner Harbor, Canton, Fells Point, Federal Hill, Mount Vernon, and Charles Village. We also cover Baltimore County communities: Towson, Dundalk, Essex, Catonsville, Pikesville, Parkville, and more. See all service areas.

Can you help with insurance documentation after glass damage?

Yes. We provide clear descriptions of the damage and work performed that you can submit to your insurance carrier. Many commercial property and homeowner policies cover sudden glass breakage, subject to your deductible and policy terms.
