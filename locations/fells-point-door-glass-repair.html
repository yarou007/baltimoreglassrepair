Look beyond the cracked panel

A crack in an entry door can be a glass issue, a door-system issue or both. For a Fells Point home or business, describe whether the door closes normally, whether the frame has moved and where the damage appears. Do not repeatedly operate a damaged glass door to demonstrate the problem.

Door glass is not interchangeable with an ordinary window pane. The opening, glazing method, hardware and applicable safety requirements influence the replacement. A decorative panel, a sidelite and a commercial door lite may each need different measurements and materials.

Check authorization and design constraints

If the property is rented, confirm whether the tenant, owner or manager can authorize the repair. If a building has historic-design requirements, determine whether an approval is needed before changing a visible door or glazing detail. A repair visit should not be treated as confirmation that a proposed design change is permitted.

Tell us about any existing pattern, tint, bevel, wire or other feature you want assessed. A replacement has to be appropriate for its application; matching appearance alone is not enough. Ask what can be retained and what requires a different specification.

Keep the entrance safe while arranging work

  • Keep people away from loose glass, exposed edges and cracked panels.
  • Avoid slamming or forcing a door that binds or no longer closes correctly.
  • Tell the property manager if an entrance or exit is affected.
  • For missing glass, ask about temporary securing and how it will affect access.
  • If you share photos on WhatsApp, take them only from a safe position and avoid unnecessary personal details.

What to discuss before booking

Explain the door type, panel location, service address and whether the property is occupied or trading. Ask about assessment, measurements, removal and disposal, glazing materials and any related hardware work. A panel that needs ordering may require a separate return visit, so discuss the interim plan as well as the final installation.
