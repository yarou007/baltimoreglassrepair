Storefront Glass Repair in Canton, Baltimore

We repair storefront glass and entry-door panels for Canton shops and restaurants. Call about an exposed opening or send photos to discuss replacement.

Separate the glass problem from the access problem

A damaged Canton shopfront can affect more than the panel. Customers may approach the entrance, deliveries may still arrive, and staff may need another safe way in. Before deciding when to replace the glass, establish whether the damaged opening is a fixed display window, a working entry door or part of a larger storefront system.

Keep people away from cracked, missing or loose glass. Do not assume an entrance is safe because the door still moves. If an exit or access route is affected, coordinate with the building manager and appropriate safety authorities before directing people to another route.

When temporary protection makes sense

A replacement panel may need confirmed dimensions or fabrication before installation. Temporary board-up can be a separate step when an opening is exposed. It is important to discuss how boarding will affect the door, visibility, business access and the eventual return for glazing.

Temporary protection does not tell you the permanent replacement specification. Ask how measurements will be taken, which parts of the existing system can be retained and what might change if the frame is also damaged. Do not promise customers a reopening time before the site and material requirements are understood.

Details that help plan storefront work

  • Identify the damaged panel: door lite, sidelite, transom or display pane.
  • Share the service address, opening hours and the person who can approve work.
  • Describe delivery access, parking and any restrictions on bringing a large panel through the property.
  • Mention frame movement, a door that will not close or damage beyond the glass.
  • Discuss how staff and customer access will be managed during assessment and installation.

Agree on a complete replacement scope

Glass type, dimensions, frame condition and safe handling affect the job. Ask whether the quote includes removal, disposal, any temporary securing and the permanent installation. Where a landlord or property manager controls the storefront, confirm authorization before work is booked. Insurance questions should be directed to the insurer; coverage cannot be assumed from the cause of breakage.
