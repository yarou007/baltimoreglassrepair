Glass Repair in Fells Point, MD

Fells Point is one of Baltimore's oldest and most vibrant neighborhoods—a waterfront entertainment district with historic cobblestone streets, bars and restaurants along Broadway and Thames Street, boutique retail, and beautifully preserved rowhomes dating to the 18th century. Glass work here requires understanding of historic window systems, tight street access, and the after-hours pace of the nightlife district.

Commercial glass in Fells Point

Fells Point's commercial corridor runs along Broadway, Thames Street, and the surrounding blocks—filled with bars, restaurants, galleries, and retail shops. Storefront glass here takes heavy use from foot traffic and urban activity. When a display window cracks or a door lite breaks, we handle it—often after hours when the entertainment district quiets down. We coordinate with business owners on timing to minimize disruption during service hours.

Residential glass in Fells Point

Fells Point rowhomes include some of Baltimore's oldest residential buildings, many with original wood-sash windows. We handle window glass replacement in these historic structures carefully—matching glass type and thickness to the existing system. We also work on modern renovations and condo conversions in the area, including shower doors, mirrors, and patio door glass.

Emergency board up in Fells Point

The nightlife district means glass emergencies can happen at any hour. We provide 24/7 board up service throughout Fells Point—securing broken storefronts, door glass, and windows against weather and intrusion. We then schedule permanent replacement once materials are confirmed.
