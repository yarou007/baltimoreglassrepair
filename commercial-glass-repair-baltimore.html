Emergency commercial glass process

When a Baltimore business calls with a glass emergency, we follow a clear, repeatable process:

  1. Call and triage: Call 703-244-0559. We ask for the business address, access point, whether the building is occupied or alarmed, and a description of the damage.
  2. Dispatch: We send a commercial glass crew with appropriate tools, board up materials, and common glass stock. ETA is typically 20–60 minutes.
  3. Secure the site: Our first priority is safety. We board up if needed, address glass shards and hazards, and assess the full scope of damage.
  4. Measure and spec: We measure the opening, determine glass type (tempered, laminated, IGU), thickness, and compatibility with existing frames and hardware.
  5. Repair or schedule replacement: Standard sizes may allow same-day installation. Custom glass is ordered and installation is scheduled around your business operations.
  6. Documentation: We provide complete descriptions of damage, materials installed, and work performed for your records and insurance claims.
