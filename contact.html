What happens next?

Can I get a price over the phone?

Describe the glass and the damage. Exact pricing may require measurements and an assessment of the frame and access. Ask what the assessment involves before scheduling.

What if replacement glass needs to be ordered?

Ask about temporary protection and the expected steps for measurement, ordering and installation.

What should I do if the glass is unsafe?

Keep people and pets away from damaged glass. Do not touch loose pieces or operate a damaged glass door. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services.
