Dundalk Board-Up: Planning the Move to Permanent Glass

Understand temporary securing, replacement measurements and the handover between board-up and permanent glass work in Dundalk.

By Baltimore Glass Repair · November 13, 2026

Treat securing and replacement as separate stages

A broken opening in a Dundalk home or business may need protection before the correct glass can be installed. Board-up addresses an immediate exposed opening; it does not by itself confirm the dimensions, glass type or final installation schedule. Ask about both stages when discussing the job.

Keep people away from damaged glass. Where police, emergency services or building security are involved, coordinate access with them before repair work begins. Do not attempt to remove loose panes or unstable boarding yourself.

Make the temporary plan specific

Ask which opening will be secured, how the board will be fixed and how that changes access. If the opening is in a door or shared route, the site manager needs to understand the effect on entry and exit. Temporary protection should be assessed for the property rather than treated as the same arrangement for every opening.

Confirm who has access to the property and who will be responsible for updates. For a rental or business unit, identify who approves the work and who pays before the scope is agreed.

Confirm what is needed for the permanent panel

Replacement can require measurement of the opening, assessment of the frame and selection of the appropriate glass. A panel that appears standard may still differ in thickness, treatment or retention details. A photograph can explain the damage, but it cannot replace all measurements.

If the frame is damaged, ask how that changes the plan. Ordering glass before establishing whether the frame can retain it properly can lead to an incomplete solution.

Questions for the follow-up handover

  • Have final measurements been taken or is another assessment needed?
  • What glass specification is being ordered?
  • Which temporary work is included in the agreed price?
  • Who will confirm material availability and installation timing?
  • What needs to be cleared or arranged before the return visit?

Do not infer an installation date from the board-up

Availability, fabrication and access affect the permanent work. Ask when to expect the next update and what has to happen before the installation can be confirmed. Keep children, pets, staff and customers away from any restricted opening until it is safe to use.
