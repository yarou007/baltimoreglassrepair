Can You Tape or Seal Broken Glass Safely for One Night?

Keep people away from damaged glass overnight. Tape and plastic do not stabilize a broken pane or secure an opening. Pressing adhesive onto cracked glass can dislodge it.

When a draft is the immediate concern

Close off the room if you can do so without going near the glass. Leave a cracked pane and its frame alone until they can be assessed. If the opening is exposed to wind or rain, ask about temporary protection rather than trying to attach a cover to the window.

When to call for board up

Ground-floor commercial openings, missing lites, or weather exposure—see emergency board up and broken window safety.

Does the opening need to be secured overnight?

Call if the pane is missing, loose or easy to reach from outside. Share the location and whether the property is occupied.
