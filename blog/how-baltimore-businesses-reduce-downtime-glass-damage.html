How Baltimore Business Owners Can Reduce Downtime After Glass Damage

Commercial · Storefront glass

Downtime isn’t only “closed until fixed.” It’s confused customers, staff standing outside, and revenue walking past. In Baltimore corridors like Canton or Fells Point, a clear plan beats a perfect timeline.

Communicate one truth at a time

Post a single sentence at the door: alternate entry or temporary closure reason. Update when the board up is complete or when replacement is scheduled—avoid contradictory messages between social and signage.

Stage alternate flows

If a side door is safe and code-compliant for temporary use, say so. If not, don’t improvise—cordons exist for liability reasons.

Align with vendor reality

We’ll tell you if same-day replacement is realistic or if fabrication is required. Use that to plan staff shifts, not hope. Pair with replacement timelines.
