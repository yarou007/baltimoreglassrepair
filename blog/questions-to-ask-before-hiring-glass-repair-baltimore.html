What Questions Should You Ask Before Hiring a Glass Repair Company in Baltimore?

Use the same photo and description with each company. Ask for the assessment, temporary protection and final glass work to be priced separately where they apply, so you can compare the same work.

  • How do you verify safety glazing requirements for door openings?
  • What’s your realistic ETA range for my address tonight?
  • Do you separate emergency secure work from permanent replacement on invoices?
  • How do you measure odd storefront frames?
  • Can you replace only the damaged glass? What would make a new frame necessary?

Before authorizing work, confirm what the quote includes for removal, disposal and any follow-up visit. Ask what could change the scope once the opening is measured.
