What Is the Fastest Temporary Fix for a Broken Window Before Help Arrives?

First, keep people and pets away from the window. Close the room if you can do so safely. Leave any glass held by the frame in place.

A missing pane on the ground floor can leave the home open to weather and entry. Ask about temporary board-up if the window cannot secure or if glass might fall onto a walkway. If the pane is still in place, avoid opening or closing the sash until it can be assessed.
