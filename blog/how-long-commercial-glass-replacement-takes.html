How Long Does Commercial Glass Replacement Take in Baltimore?

Commercial · Timelines

Timing depends on the panel and the opening. A standard door pane may be available sooner than an oversized storefront panel that needs fabrication. Ask for separate timing for securing an exposed opening, ordering glass and returning to install it.

Stock vs custom

Supplier inventory, fabrication and tempering can affect when a panel is ready. If the opening is exposed while you wait, ask about temporary board-up.

Hardware and access

Closer adjustments, panic devices, and wind loads aren’t “glass-only” problems. If the door needs work beyond the lite, we flag it early.

Weather

Sealants and install conditions for exterior work can shift schedules—especially in shoulder seasons along waterfront-adjacent sites.

Ask about timing for your opening

Share the type of glass, the number of damaged panels and when we can access the site. Tell us if an opening still needs to be secured.
