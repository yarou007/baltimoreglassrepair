What should I do immediately after glass breaks?

Keep people and pets away from the area. Wear thick gloves if you must handle glass. Do not lean on damaged frames. If weather or security is a concern, call 703-244-0559 for emergency service. Take photos of the damage before cleanup for insurance documentation.

Can I tape broken glass as a temporary fix?

Tape can sometimes hold cracked annealed glass temporarily, but it does not work on tempered glass (which shatters completely) and creates false confidence on compromised panels. For safety, call for professional boarding or replacement rather than relying on tape.

Board up service

What is the difference between board up and full glass replacement?

Board up temporarily secures a broken opening with plywood or polycarbonate to protect against weather and intrusion. Full replacement installs the permanent new lite. Board up is often the first step when replacement glass needs to be ordered or fabricated.

How long can a board up safely protect my property?

Board up is designed as temporary protection for days to a couple of weeks while glass is fabricated. It is not a permanent solution. Weather, tampering, and foot traffic affect durability. We schedule replacement as quickly as materials allow.

Do you repair storefront glass in Baltimore?

What types of commercial glass do you work with?

We work with tempered glass, laminated glass, insulated glass units (IGUs), low-E coated glass, wired glass, and specialty safety glazing. We verify the correct product for each opening based on code requirements and building specs.

Can a cracked commercial glass door be repaired?

Many commercial door lites use tempered glass, which shatters into small pieces when compromised. Cracked tempered panels typically require full replacement for safety and code compliance. We evaluate in person—see door glass repair.

What causes storefront glass to break?

Common causes include vandalism, attempted break-ins, vehicle impact, severe weather (wind-blown debris, hail), thermal stress from direct sun exposure, impact from deliveries or equipment, and settling in older buildings.

Residential glass

Can foggy double-pane windows be fixed?

Foggy windows mean the insulating seal between panes has failed. The glass unit (IGU) typically needs replacement rather than repair. We measure and order the correct unit—the sash and frame can often be reused, saving you the cost of full window replacement.

Do you repair patio door glass?

Yes. We repair and replace glass in sliding patio doors, French doors, and hinged patio doors. This includes tempered safety glass panels and insulated glass units. Same-day service is available for common sizes when materials are in stock.

How do I know if my shower door needs repair or replacement?

If the glass is cracked, chipped, or shattered, it needs replacement—shower doors use tempered glass that cannot be repaired once compromised. If the issue is hardware-related (loose hinges, worn seals, sticking door), repair may be possible without replacing the glass.

Safety glass & product types

What is tempered glass and where is it required?

Tempered glass is heat-treated to be 4–5 times stronger than regular glass. When it breaks, it shatters into small granular pieces rather than sharp shards. Building codes require tempered glass in doors, sidelites, shower enclosures, and near walking surfaces where impact risk exists.

What is the difference between tempered and laminated glass?

Tempered glass shatters into small pieces on impact. Laminated glass has a plastic interlayer that holds fragments together when broken, providing better security and sound reduction. Laminated is often used in security applications, skylights, and areas where glass retention after breakage is important.
