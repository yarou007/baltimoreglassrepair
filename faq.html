Glass repair & replacement

How soon can you get here?

Call +1 202-643-4062 for current availability and an ETA for your address. Timing depends on location, access, traffic and the work required.

Can broken glass always be replaced immediately?

Some panels need measurements or fabrication before replacement. If the opening is exposed, ask about temporary board-up while the correct glass is arranged.

What affects the price?

Glass dimensions, glass type, frame condition, access and urgency all affect the scope. Call to describe the damage and discuss assessment and pricing.

What should I do while waiting?

Keep people and pets away from damaged glass. Do not touch loose pieces or operate a damaged glass door. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services.

Do I need to replace the whole window?

Not always. Some frames and sashes can accept replacement glass. Frame condition, glass specifications and the window system determine the options.

What does fog between window panes mean?

Moisture between panes can indicate a failed seal in an insulated glass unit. Cleaning the outside will not remove moisture inside a sealed unit.

Do you handle homes and businesses?

Service options include residential windows and door glass, commercial storefronts and temporary board-up. Call with your location and the type of work.

Does my insurance cover broken glass?

Coverage depends on your policy, the cause of damage and any deductible. Photograph the damage from a safe distance and contact your insurer about its requirements.

Before a commercial service visit

  • Confirm who can approve the work.
  • Share access, parking and building restrictions.
  • Keep staff and visitors away from damaged glass.
  • Ask about assessment, scope, pricing and timing.
