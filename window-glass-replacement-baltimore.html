Common window glass problems

Cracked or broken panes

If the sash and frame are in good condition, replacing the affected glass may be enough. Keep people away from cracks and loose pieces.

Fog between double panes

Moisture between panes can mean the insulated unit has lost its seal. An assessment can determine whether the unit can be replaced within the sash.
