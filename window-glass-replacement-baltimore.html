Window glass problems we solve

Our window glass replacement service addresses the most common residential and commercial window issues across Baltimore:

  • Cracked window panes: Small cracks spread with temperature swings. A cracked pane is both a security risk and an energy drain. We assess whether the lite can be replaced in the sash or whether the whole unit needs to be ordered.
  • Fogged glass between panes: When moisture appears between double-pane windows, the insulated glass unit's seal has failed. The fix is IGU replacement—not "defogging" services that rarely last. We measure and install the correct replacement unit.
  • Broken windows from impact: Ball impacts, falling branches, construction debris, and break-ins that shatter window glass. We provide 24/7 emergency response with board up and replacement scheduling.
  • Single pane glass replacement: Many older Baltimore rowhomes still have single pane windows. We replace broken single pane glass in existing sashes, including proper glazing and putty work.
  • Double pane IGU replacement: Modern windows use insulated glass units for energy efficiency. We replace failed or broken IGUs with correctly specified units that match your window system.
  • Patio door glass: Sliding patio door glass and French door glass replacement with tempered safety glass as required by code.

Commercial window glass replacement

Commercial buildings have different window glass requirements than residential properties. Ground-floor windows in retail and office buildings may require tempered or laminated safety glass. Energy codes may mandate specific U-values and Solar Heat Gain Coefficients. We work with building managers and property owners to ensure window glass replacements meet all applicable codes and performance requirements.

For broader commercial glass work including storefronts, entry doors, and interior glass, see our dedicated commercial page.

Same-day vs scheduled window replacement

We handle both emergency and scheduled window glass replacement:

  • Same-day replacement is available for common window sizes and standard glass configurations that we carry in stock. If your window uses a standard-size IGU or single pane, we can often replace it the same day you call.
  • Scheduled replacement is needed for custom-sized windows, specialty glass types, and insulated glass units that require fabrication. We board up the opening for security and weather protection while the correct glass is being made.

Window glass types we install

  • Single pane clear glass — for older sash windows and storm windows
  • Double pane insulated glass (IGU) — for energy-efficient modern windows
  • Tempered safety glass — required by code for specific locations (near doors, low windows, bathrooms)
  • Laminated glass — enhanced security and sound dampening for urban environments
  • Low-E coated glass — improved thermal performance and UV protection
  • Obscured/patterned glass — privacy glass for bathrooms and side windows

Window Glass Replacement FAQ

How much does window glass replacement cost in Baltimore?

Cost depends on the glass type (single pane, double pane IGU, tempered, laminated), window size, accessibility, and whether the sash or frame needs work. We provide upfront scope and pricing before starting—no hidden fees. Call for a free estimate on scheduled, non-emergency work.

Can you replace fogged double-pane windows?

Yes. Fogging between panes means the insulated glass unit's seal has failed and moisture has entered. The fix is replacement of the IGU—not "defogging." We measure and order the correct unit to restore clarity and insulation.

Do you replace both single pane and double pane windows?

Yes. We replace single pane glass in older Baltimore rowhomes and apartments, and double pane insulated glass units in newer construction. We match the existing glass type, thickness, and frame requirements.

Can you do same-day window glass replacement?

Same-day replacement is possible for common sizes and standard configurations. Custom-sized windows and IGUs may require 1–3 business days for fabrication. We board up the window for security while waiting.

Do you handle emergency window repair after storms?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency response for broken windows after storms. We board up the window to protect against weather and intrusion, then schedule replacement once the correct glass is available.
