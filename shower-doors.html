Before you call about shower glass

Shower glass and enclosure fit

Framed shower doors

Tell us which panel is damaged and whether the frame or track has shifted. The condition of the enclosure affects what can be reused.

Frameless shower panels

Replacement panels need to fit the opening, hinges and cutouts and meet the enclosure’s safety glass requirements.

Doors that bind or leak

Alignment, hinges and seals can affect how a shower door works. We assess the cause before discussing a panel replacement.
