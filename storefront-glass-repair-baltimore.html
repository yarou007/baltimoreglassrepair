Storefront glass services we provide

Our storefront glass repair covers the full range of commercial glass work that Baltimore businesses need:

  • Display windows and vision lites: Large glass panels that form the face of your storefront. We measure and spec the correct thickness, tempering, and tint to match existing glass.
  • Entry door glass: Tempered door lites, commercial entry doors, and automatic door glass. Includes hardware compatibility checks and safety glazing verification.
  • Sidelites: The narrow glass panels flanking entry doors. These often require specific dimensions and tempering standards.
  • Aluminum storefront framing: When the frame itself is damaged—bent by impact or warped during a break-in—we assess whether repair or replacement of the frame section is needed.
  • Transom glass: The glass above entry doors that completes the storefront system. Often overlooked during damage assessments but critical for weather sealing.

Common storefront glass problems in Baltimore

Baltimore's mix of historic commercial districts, high-traffic retail areas, and nightlife zones creates unique storefront glass challenges:

  • Vandalism and forced entry — shattered display windows and pried-open door glass, especially in areas around Downtown Baltimore, the Inner Harbor, and Fells Point after hours
  • Impact damage — vehicles, delivery equipment, and construction activity near storefronts along busy corridors in Canton, Harbor East, and Federal Hill
  • Storm damage — wind-driven debris, fallen branches, and severe weather that cracks or shatters large vision lites
  • Age and seal failure — older storefront glass losing its seal, causing fogging between panes and reduced energy efficiency

Board up vs immediate storefront replacement

Not every storefront glass emergency can be resolved with same-day replacement. Large vision lites, custom-tinted panels, and oversized configurations often require fabrication lead time. When the correct glass isn't available immediately, we provide professional board up service to:

  • Secure the opening against weather and unauthorized entry
  • Maintain a clean, professional appearance for your business
  • Allow your business to continue operating while waiting for replacement glass

We then return to install the correctly sized and specified replacement glass as soon as it's ready.

Storefront glass types we install

The correct glass type matters for safety, code compliance, and energy performance:

  • Tempered safety glass — required by code for most storefront applications; breaks into small, relatively safe cubes rather than dangerous shards
  • Laminated glass — holds together when shattered thanks to an interlayer film; provides enhanced security and sound dampening
  • Insulated glass units (IGUs) — double or triple pane configurations for improved energy efficiency in heated and cooled commercial spaces
  • Low-E coated glass — reduces UV transmission and improves thermal performance for retail displays and office spaces

Storefront Glass Repair FAQ

How quickly can you repair broken storefront glass in Baltimore?

For standard storefront glass sizes that we carry in stock, same-day repair is often possible. Custom-sized vision lites and oversized panels may require fabrication lead time—typically 1–3 business days. We board up the opening immediately while the replacement glass is being prepared.

Do you repair aluminum storefront frames?

Yes. If the aluminum frame has been bent or damaged during a break-in or impact, we assess whether the frame can be straightened and reglazed or whether a new frame section is needed. We work with standard commercial aluminum storefront systems.

What types of storefront glass do you install?

We install tempered safety glass, laminated glass, insulated glass units (IGUs), and low-E coated glass. The correct type depends on building codes, the storefront location, and your security and energy requirements.

Can you minimize business downtime during storefront glass repair?

Yes. We coordinate installation around your business hours when possible. For emergencies, we secure the opening with a board up to keep your business operational while waiting for the replacement glass.

Is storefront glass repair covered by commercial insurance?

Many commercial property policies cover sudden glass breakage from vandalism, storms, or accidents—subject to your deductible and policy terms. We provide detailed documentation of the damage and work performed for insurance claims.
