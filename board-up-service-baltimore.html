When board up is the right first step

Board up is recommended before glass replacement in these common situations:

  • Vandalism and break-ins: The opening needs to be secured immediately to prevent further theft, damage, or unauthorized access. Police may need to complete their investigation before glass can be installed.
  • Storm damage: Severe weather can break glass when suppliers are closed and replacement panels are not available for immediate installation.
  • Custom glass sizing: Large storefront vision lites, oversized door panels, and specialty glass configurations require fabrication lead time—typically 1–3 business days.
  • After-hours emergencies: When damage occurs at night or on weekends and glass suppliers are not available until the next business day.
  • Overnight security: Property managers and business owners who need to secure an opening overnight while scheduling morning replacement.
  • Insurance processing: Some insurance carriers require documentation and approval before authorizing replacement, creating a gap that board up fills.

Our board up process

When you call 703-244-0559 for board up service, here's what to expect:

  1. Phone assessment: We ask about the opening size, location, and any safety concerns to bring the right materials and crew.
  2. Fast dispatch: A crew is dispatched with plywood, fasteners, and tools. ETA is typically 20–60 minutes.
  3. Site safety: We address immediate hazards—loose glass shards, structural concerns, and any safety issues for occupants or pedestrians.
  4. Professional board up: The opening is secured with properly cut plywood, fastened securely to prevent removal, and sealed against weather infiltration.
  5. Measurement for replacement: While on-site, we measure the opening and spec the correct replacement glass so the order can be placed immediately.
  6. Replacement scheduling: We coordinate glass fabrication and schedule the permanent installation, then remove the board up during the install.

Board up vs immediate glass replacement

The decision between board up and immediate replacement depends on glass availability, time of day, and the type of glass needed:

  • Same-day replacement is possible when we have the correct glass size in stock, the opening is a standard configuration, and the damage occurs during supplier hours.
  • Board up is necessary when the glass requires custom fabrication, the damage occurs after hours, or the opening is large enough that rush-fabricated glass would be the wrong spec.

We never push board up when same-day replacement is genuinely possible—and we never rush a replacement with the wrong glass just to skip the board up step. Getting the glass right matters more than getting it fast.

Commercial vs residential board up

Commercial board up often involves storefronts, entry doors, and display windows where appearance, business continuity, and public safety are priorities. We work with property managers, business owners, and multi-tenant building operators to coordinate access and minimize disruption.

Residential board up priorities are different—security, heat retention in winter, and keeping children and pets safe from exposed openings. We secure window openings and door glass openings with the same professional materials and fastening.

Board Up Service FAQ

When should I choose board up instead of immediate glass replacement?

Board up is the right choice when replacement glass is not available immediately—due to custom sizing, after-hours supplier closures, oversized fabrication, or when the correct glass type needs to be ordered. Board up secures the opening while the permanent replacement is being prepared.

How long does emergency board up take?

Most single-opening board up jobs are completed within 30–60 minutes after our crew arrives on site. Larger openings or multiple openings may take longer. We give you a clear time estimate on arrival.

Is board up available 24/7 in Baltimore?

Yes. We provide emergency board up service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Baltimore City, Baltimore County, and surrounding areas. Nights, weekends, and holidays are covered.

How much does emergency board up cost?

Will you also handle the glass replacement after board up?

Yes. Board up is typically the first step in our two-step process. After securing the opening, we measure for the correct replacement glass, coordinate the order, and schedule installation. We handle everything from board up through final glass replacement.
