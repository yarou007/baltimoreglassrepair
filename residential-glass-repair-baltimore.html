Broken home windows

A broken window in a Baltimore rowhome, condo, or detached home creates immediate problems—weather exposure, security risk, and noise. Whether the damage came from a storm, an accident, a break-in, or even a stray ball, we handle the repair or replacement quickly and correctly.

We assess whether the glass can be replaced in the existing sash or whether the frame also needs attention. For standard single-pane windows, we often carry stock that allows same-day repair. For double-pane insulated glass units (IGUs), we measure accurately and order the correct unit to restore both clarity and insulation.

If the broken window creates an immediate security or weather concern, we can board up the opening first and schedule replacement once materials arrive.

Foggy double-pane glass

Condensation or haze between the panes of a double-pane window means the insulating seal has failed. This isn't a cosmetic issue—it reduces energy efficiency and can worsen over time. The fix is straightforward: we remove the failed insulated glass unit (IGU) and install a new one.

In many cases, the window sash and frame are still in good condition. We don't push full window replacement when a glass-only swap will solve the problem. We measure the unit, order the correct IGU with the right spacer and gas fill, and install it cleanly.

Foggy windows are especially common in older Baltimore homes that received window upgrades 10–20 years ago. The seals on those original IGUs eventually fail, and the glass clouds. If you're seeing this in multiple windows, we can assess and quote the full scope.

Patio door glass

Patio door glass—whether sliding, French, or hinged—takes constant use and is often the largest single lite in a home. When patio door glass breaks or develops a seal failure, it's both a safety and comfort issue. All patio door glass uses tempered safety glass, which shatters into small pieces rather than sharp shards.

We replace patio door glass panels, including insulated units for energy-efficient doors. We check the roller and track system on sliding doors and verify that the frame and hardware are compatible with the new glass. Same-day replacement is available for common configurations when materials are in stock.

Shower doors and mirrors

We install and replace both frameless and framed shower doors, shower enclosures, and glass panels for tub surrounds. All shower glass is tempered safety glass as required by building code. We also cut and install custom mirrors for bathrooms, bedrooms, hallways, closets, and decorative applications.

Common shower door issues include cracked or chipped glass, failing hardware and hinges, worn-out sweeps and seals, and glass that no longer sits square in the opening. We evaluate whether the hardware can be serviced or whether a full glass replacement makes more sense.

When repair is possible vs. replacement

Not every glass problem requires full replacement. Here's a practical guide:

  • Small chips or nicks in non-safety glass: May be repairable depending on location and size
  • Cracked tempered glass: Always requires replacement—tempered glass is designed to shatter completely when compromised
  • Foggy double-pane: The IGU needs replacement; the frame often stays
  • Scratched glass: Deep scratches usually mean replacement; surface scratches may be polished in some cases
  • Failed shower door hardware: Hardware may be replaceable without changing the glass
  • Broken window sash: The sash itself may need replacement, not just the glass

When in doubt, call 703-244-0559 and describe what you're looking at. We can often help you decide over the phone whether you need a same-day emergency visit or can schedule a convenient appointment.

A homeowner in Canton called after a storm knocked a tree branch into a second-floor bedroom window. The single-pane glass was shattered and rain was coming in. We arrived within 45 minutes with board-up materials and common glass stock.

On-site, we found the window frame was intact but the glass was completely gone. Because the opening matched a standard size we had on the truck, we installed the replacement pane the same visit—no board-up needed. For non-standard sizes, we would have boarded the opening, measured precisely, and scheduled the install within a few business days once the glass was fabricated.

Residential Glass Repair FAQ

How much does residential glass repair cost in Baltimore?

Costs depend on glass type, size, and whether the frame needs work. Single-pane window repairs are typically less expensive than double-pane IGU replacements. Call 703-244-0559 for a free estimate on scheduled work—we'll provide clear pricing before any work begins.

Can foggy double-pane windows be repaired or do they need full replacement?

Fogged insulated glass units (IGUs) mean the seal between panes has failed. In most cases, the glass unit needs replacement rather than repair. We measure and order the correct IGU to restore clarity and insulation. The sash and frame can often be reused.

Do you repair patio door glass in Baltimore?

Yes. We repair and replace glass in sliding patio doors, French doors, and hinged patio doors. This includes tempered safety glass panels and insulated glass units. Same-day service is available for common sizes when materials are in stock.

How quickly can you replace a broken window in a Baltimore rowhome?

For common window sizes, same-day replacement is often possible when materials are in stock and scheduling allows. Custom or oversized windows may require ordering. We can board up the opening first if security or weather is a concern. Call 703-244-0559 for a real-time ETA.

Do you handle shower door glass and mirrors?

Yes. We install and replace frameless and framed shower doors, shower enclosures, and custom-cut mirrors for bathrooms, bedrooms, and living spaces. All shower door glass is tempered safety glass as required by code.
