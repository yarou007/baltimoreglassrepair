Door glass services we provide

Our door glass repair service covers the full range of door glass work that Baltimore homeowners and businesses need:

  • Entry door glass: Front door glass lites, half-lites, and full-lite door panels for residential homes. We replace the glass without replacing the door, matching tempered glass requirements and decorative patterns when possible.
  • Sidelite glass: The narrow glass panels flanking entry doors. Sidelites require specific dimensions and tempered glass. We measure and install replacement sidelite glass that matches your door system.
  • Commercial entry door glass: Tempered glass in commercial entry doors, aluminum-framed doors, and automatic door systems. Includes hardware compatibility checks and ADA compliance considerations for commercial properties.
  • Patio door glass: Sliding glass door panels, French door glass, and hinged patio door glass. Tempered safety glass is required by code for all patio door applications.
  • Storm door glass: Replacement glass and screen panels for aluminum and vinyl storm doors. Common sizes are often available for same-day replacement.
  • Glass insert panels: Decorative glass inserts, beveled glass, and textured glass panels in residential front doors.

Door glass emergencies

Broken door glass creates immediate security and safety problems. A shattered front door exposes your home to weather and intruders. A broken commercial entry door puts employees, customers, and inventory at risk. We provide 24/7 emergency response for door glass emergencies across Baltimore.

When door glass breaks after hours, we secure the opening with a professional board up and schedule replacement for the earliest possible time. For standard door glass sizes, same-day replacement is often possible.

Safety glazing requirements for doors

Building codes require tempered or laminated safety glass in door applications. This is not optional—regular annealed glass in a door creates a serious injury risk if the door is slammed, hit by wind, or walked into. We verify that every door glass installation meets local safety glazing requirements, including:

  • All glass within doors must be tempered or laminated
  • Sidelites adjacent to doors require safety glazing
  • Glass within 24 inches of a door's edge that extends to within 60 inches of the floor requires safety glazing
  • Patio door glass and sliding door glass must be tempered

Door Glass Repair FAQ

Can you replace just the glass in my door without replacing the whole door?

In most cases, yes. If the door frame and hardware are intact, we can replace just the glass lite—the glass panel within the door. This is more cost-effective than full door replacement and maintains your existing hardware and finish.

What type of glass is used in doors?

Building codes require tempered safety glass in most door applications. Tempered glass is approximately four times stronger than regular glass and breaks into small, relatively safe cubes rather than dangerous shards. Some applications may use laminated glass for added security.

How long does door glass replacement take?

Standard door glass replacement typically takes 1–2 hours on-site once the correct glass is available. Common sizes can often be replaced same-day. Custom-sized or specialty door glass may require 1–3 business days for fabrication.

Do you repair sliding glass door glass?

Yes. We replace broken and fogged glass in sliding patio doors, including tempered safety glass panels and insulated glass units. We work with standard sliding door systems and can measure for custom sizes.

Can you fix door glass after a break-in?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency response for broken door glass after break-ins. We secure the opening immediately—with board up if replacement glass is not available—and coordinate replacement as quickly as possible.
